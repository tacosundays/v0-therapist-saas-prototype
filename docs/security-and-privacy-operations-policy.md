# Security and Privacy Operations Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026  
Owner: SessionSteps LLC  
Status: Launch draft pending owner approval and qualified legal/security review.

## Access control

- Grant access only for a documented business need and use the least privilege necessary.
- Require unique accounts, strong authentication, and MFA for administrative systems.
- Never share passwords or recovery codes. Store secrets only in approved secret managers.
- Review production, vendor, and Workspace access quarterly and after every workforce change.
- Remove access immediately when a person leaves or no longer requires it; preserve the audit record.

## Workstation and remote-work safeguards

- Use supported operating systems, automatic security updates, disk encryption, screen locks, and current malware protection.
- Do not download PHI to unmanaged or shared devices. Avoid public computers and untrusted networks.
- Report lost devices, suspicious logins, misdirected messages, or possible data exposure immediately.

## Minimum necessary and approved channels

- Access and disclose only the information needed for the task.
- Do not put PHI in Stripe payment fields, analytics tools, ordinary support email, advertising tools, or unapproved integrations.
- Verify the recipient before sending sensitive information and use approved PHI-capable services only after contractual eligibility is documented.

## Privacy requests

- Route requests to privacy@sessionsteps.com and record the requester, workspace, request type, date, identity-verification method, owner, and outcome.
- Confirm whether the request must be handled by the customer practice as the record controller or by SessionSteps under contract.
- Do not disclose or delete records until identity and authority are verified.
- Preserve information subject to legal, security, billing, or contractual holds; explain any limitation.
- Record completion and notify the requester through an appropriate channel.

## Vendor management

- Before a vendor receives PHI, document its purpose, data categories, security review, location, minimum configuration, subprocessor exposure, and signed BAA or other required terms.
- Reassess vendors annually and when services, ownership, terms, or data use materially change.
- Disable integrations that lose required eligibility or cannot meet SessionSteps requirements.
- Keep the vendor BAA register and public Subprocessor List current.

## Changes and training

The owner approves this policy, trains anyone with administrative or support access before granting access, and retains evidence of training and policy acknowledgements. Exceptions require written risk acceptance and an expiration date.
